Text Relay

Every text that reaches one Android phone, RCS included, read and answered on another, even one without Google services. Scan once; both apps then install and update from F-Droid.

Add the repository

  1. In F-Droid, go to Settings, Repositories, tap +, then Scan QR code. Clients with a QR button on the main screen work the same way.
  2. Scan the code above. Adding the repository installs nothing on its own.
  3. Search for Relay or Texts and tap Install. Updates arrive the same way.

The two apps

  • Relay On the phone that keeps your SIM and Google Messages

    Forwards every text, SMS, MMS and RCS, groups and full-size pictures included, to your Text Relay inbox, and sends your replies back out through Messages from that number. Leave it on Wi-Fi and a charger.

    Download the APK directly
  • Texts On the phone you carry

    Reads and answers every conversation, with a notification for each one as texts arrive. Works with no Google services, push app or browser.

    Download the APK directly

Each app pairs with a one-time code made in the other: in Texts, Settings, Pair the relay device; in Relay, Pair a phone with Texts. Scan its QR code with the new phone's camera, or type the code. A direct download installs the same build F-Droid serves; Android asks once to allow installs from your browser, and on phones with Google Play, Play Protect may ask you to confirm with More details, Install anyway.
